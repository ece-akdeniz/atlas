# Atlas design system

Task: identify a worthwhile opportunity, understand its fit and uncertainty, inspect the publisher, and act.

The user requested Impeccable (https://impeccable.style/). This implementation applies its public typeset, distill and polish guidance; it does not install global hooks or claim an installed plugin.

- Product name: Atlas. The restored eight-ray star with a compact wordmark; no decorative slogan.
- Home: up to three evidence-backed recommendations before secondary navigation. No headline statistics, city quotas or filler.
- Type: system sans serif, 16px body; 28–32px page headings; 19–23px content headings; 13–15px supporting content. Readable line lengths; wrapping titles rather than truncation.
- Layout: persistent desktop navigation, compact mobile header, fully spelled category controls. Spacing follows an 8px rhythm with 14–24px component gaps.
- Light: cool off-white, white surfaces, restrained forest accent.
- Dark (user preference): neutral dark-gray canvas #19191d, raised surface #222227, plum accent #d1a4df with deep plum foreground #2b1235. No green-tinted dark backgrounds.
- Semantic tokens govern text, surfaces, border, focus, warning, danger, accent and shadows. System/Light/Dark preference is applied before styles paint.
- Content: short actions, one specific fit explanation, consequential uncertainty visible. Requirements live in details; diagnostics are expandable.
- Profile: readable sections first; focused editors with Save changes and Cancel; private conditions collapsed.
- States: do not imply verification after a failed fetch, eligibility from silence, or recruitment from an interesting lab page.
- Validate in both themes at 1360px and 390px, including long titles, keyboard focus, errors and empty states.
